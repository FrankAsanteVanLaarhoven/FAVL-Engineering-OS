description: Perform a FAVL Engineering OS compliance review of the current change.
---

Review the current diff against the constitution and applicable standards. Report:
1. requirement and acceptance-criteria coverage;
2. correctness and regression risks;
3. security, privacy and data-governance risks;
4. architecture and maintainability concerns;
5. test and evidence gaps;
6. failures or uncertainty that must be disclosed;
7. exact blocking issues versus non-blocking recommendations.
Do not modify files unless explicitly asked.
