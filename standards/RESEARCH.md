# Research and Evidence Standard

For empirical or research work, preserve:
- hypothesis and falsifiable claims;
- experimental design, variables, controls, datasets, seeds, hardware, software and protocol versions;
- preregistered or predeclared metrics where appropriate;
- raw and processed result provenance;
- failures, negative results, blocked runs, sensitivity reversals, disagreement, stop conditions, and corrective decisions;
- uncertainty, alternative explanations, limitations, and non-generalisation boundaries.

Compatibility with community baselines must be preserved unless incompatibility is itself an explicit, justified contribution.
