# FAVL Engineering Constitution

## Article I — Purpose
FAVL systems shall produce verifiable real-world value while protecting people, data, infrastructure, research integrity, and institutional trust.

## Article II — Truthfulness
No contributor or agent may invent evidence, results, execution status, citations, provenance, or assurance. Uncertainty and failure must be stated precisely.

## Article III — Engineering integrity
Every material change must be reviewable, testable, reversible where practical, and attributable. Expediency does not waive safety or evidence requirements.

## Article IV — Security and privacy
Least privilege, data minimisation, secure defaults, explicit trust boundaries, protected secrets, and auditable access are mandatory.

## Article V — Human authority
Automated agents advise and execute within delegated bounds. Humans retain authority over irreversible, legally consequential, safety-critical, financial, privacy-sensitive, and production-impacting decisions.

## Article VI — Change control
Constitutional changes require an ADR, impact analysis, owner approval, version increment, and passing governance validation. Silent weakening is prohibited.

## Article VII — Ownership and attribution
Every repository, commit, document, metadata field, release note, generated artefact and public project file shows one identity:

    Frank Asante Van Laarhoven <frankleroyvan@gmail.com>

No third-party contributor name, credit line, co-author trailer, tool or vendor branding, automation identity, model identity, origination note or "produced-with" note may be added or preserved without the owner's explicit written approval. This applies to commit authorship, commit messages, file contents, workflow identities and package metadata alike.

Only Git-tracked and staged project files are in scope. Build logs, system logs, robotics logs and temporary outputs are not attribution evidence.

## Article VIII — Precedence
This constitution supersedes project convenience. Project rules may become stricter but may not weaken these articles without an approved exception record.
